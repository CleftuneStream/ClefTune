ClefTune

An unofficial YouTube Music client: ad-free playback, synced lyrics, offline downloads and your own library.

On the next screens you can choose how it opens:

  • Separate window (ticked by default): ClefTune gets its own window.
  • Untick it to use your web browser instead. You can add the window later by opening this installer again.

Everything it needs is included; nothing else has to be installed.
